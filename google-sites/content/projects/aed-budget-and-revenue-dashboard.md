AED Budget and Revenue Dashboard

This private working project focuses on budget and revenue analytics in Power BI. The goal is to turn financial data into a dashboard experience that supports monitoring, variance review, and management-level reporting.

## What I built

The project centers on a governed reporting model rather than a one-off visual. That means shaping source data, defining useful measures, organizing budget and revenue views, and making the report understandable for stakeholders who need fast answers.

## Analytics value

Budget dashboards are useful when they help people move from static reports to active questions:

- Where are actuals moving away from expectations?
- Which budget areas need attention?
- How are revenue patterns changing over time?
- Which slices of the organization need clearer follow-up?
