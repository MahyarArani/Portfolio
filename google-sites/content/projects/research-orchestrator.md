Research Orchestrator

Research Orchestrator is a private CLI-first platform for organizing research discovery and proposal generation across applied data-science domains. It is designed around agentic workflows, structured outputs, and repeatable research planning.

## What it does

The system coordinates specialized agents for source discovery, paper ingestion, research analysis, gap detection, topic generation, proposal planning, peer review, debate, and context management.

## Why it is useful

Research work can become scattered quickly: papers, notes, candidate topics, methods, datasets, and dissertation planning often live in different places. This project turns that process into a structured pipeline with schemas, run history, memory, and review loops.

## Agentic AI relevance

The project is a working example of agentic AI used for knowledge work rather than simple chat. The focus is not just generating text; it is coordinating evidence, enforcing structure, tracking decisions, and helping research ideas mature into executable projects.
